# Render scene photographs

The photographed render scenes are 360° HDR photographs from Poly Haven (https://polyhaven.com), released under
CC0 (public domain). They were made into these files by `tools/make_scenes.py` (each `.hdr` the lighting at 1024 × 512,
each `.jpg` the backdrop at 4096 × 2048).
