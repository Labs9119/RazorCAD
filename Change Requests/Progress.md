### Where to find it (Changes 1)
- **Rollback bar:** the blue line at the bottom of the tree. Drag it up, or right-click a feature ▸ Roll Back / Roll to End. New features are inserted above it.
- **Reordering:** drag a feature in the tree. Its absorbed sketch moves with it, and a move that would break a reference is refused with the reason.
- **Reference arrows:** select a feature in the tree. Blue arrows come from what it uses; purple arrows go to what uses it.
- **Thin extrude:** the Thin Feature group in the Extrude panel (thickness; direction: one side, other side, mid-plane). Open sketches switch it on automatically.
- **Delete Body / Subtract Bodies:** Features tab. **Delete Surface / Move/Copy Surfaces:** Surfaces tab.
- **Cut scope:** the Feature Scope group in Extruded/Revolve Cut. Untick All bodies and click the bodies to cut.
- **Revolve axis:** click a sketch line, a straight model edge or a cylindrical face in the view.
- **Chamfer:** Chamfer type (equal, distance + angle, two distances) and Flip direction.
- **Sketch:**
  - Mirror: select the entities plus a centerline.
  - Sketch Fillet / Sketch Chamfer: set the value, then click corners.
  - Intersection Curve.
  - Orientation: rotate 90° in the sketch panel.
  - Enter no longer leaves the sketch. Esc ends the active tool first, then closes the sketch and keeps it.
- **Under-defined sketches** show "(-)" in the tree. **Show/Hide sketch:** right-click it in the tree.
- **Feature without a sketch** (for example Extrude): pick a plane or face, sketch, exit, and the feature continues.
- **Tabs:**
  - New, Open and Sample open in a new tab.
  - ✕ closes a tab and asks to save.
  - Closing RazorCAD asks to save all unsaved parts.
- **STEP/STL:**
  - File ▸ Open or Import offers a new part, inserting it in place, or inserting and locating it with mates.
  - A mates locate becomes a "Locate …" move under the import.
- **Bodies:**
  - Right-click (tree or view) ▸ Hide, Isolate, Add to New Folder / Move to folder.
  - View context menu ▸ Show Hidden Bodies.
  - Multi-body imports are grouped automatically.
- **Visibility filter:** the eye button on the view toolbar (or View ▸ Visibility…).
- **Measure:** Evaluate tab, Tools menu or the view context menu.
- **Save / Save As / Rebuild:** the buttons next to the menus.
- **Convert closed surfaces to a solid:** right-click a surface body ▸ Convert to Solid (Knit).

### Where to find it (Changes 2)
- **Shortcut badges:** the sketch tool buttons show their key (S, L, R, C, A, P, T, D, E, O, Q, Del).
- **Sweep and Sweep Cut** (Features tab):
  - Profile: a sketch, or "Circular" with a diameter (springs).
  - Path: a sketch chain or a helix.
  - Thin option.
- **Loft and Loft Cut:**
  - Profiles: two or more sketches, in order. A third (or more) profile shapes the middle.
  - A circle is split into as many equal arcs as the other profile has corners.
  - Different segment counts give an error naming the sketches.
  - Options: straight (ruled) sides, Thin.
- **Helix** (Features tab):
  - Pick a sketch with a circle (it sets the axis and diameter).
  - Define it by pitch and revolutions, pitch and height, or height and revolutions.
  - Options: start angle, clockwise, reverse.
- **Thread Cut / Thread Extrude:**
  - Click a bore or shaft; the size is picked automatically, or choose a metric or inch standard, or custom.
  - Optional pitch; left-hand option.
  - Length: whole face, depth, or up to a plane or face.
  - The bore or shaft is resized to the thread size if needed, and refused when a bore would break out of the body.
- **Fillets** where two blends exactly meet (e.g. R2 on both edges of a 4 mm wall) now give a full round.
- **Project Edges / Offset:**
  - While the tool is active, the faces the sketch plane cuts are highlighted and the cut outline is drawn in teal.
  - Clicking it converts (or offsets) the whole outline, linked to the model. Works for solids and surfaces.
- **Text** (Sketch tab, "Text"):
  - Click to place.
  - Panel: text, font (the PC's installed fonts), height (capital-letter height), angle, bold, italic. Drag to move.
  - Extrudes as a boss or cut; text inside an outline is cut out of it.
- **Wrap (Emboss / Deboss)** (Features tab):
  - Pick a sketch (outlines and/or text) and a face (flat or curved).
  - The sketch is projected along its normal onto the face and raised or sunk by the depth.

## Changes 3

### Sketches
- [x] 3.1 Involute gear tool: gear trains on reference lines between centre points, gear ratios, tolerances (backlash / clearance) for prototyping, closed profiles for extruding

### Where to find it (Changes 3)
- **Gears** (Sketch tab):
  - Draw lines between the gear centres (centerlines work well), as in the concept image.
  - Select the lines (or a single point for one gear) and click Gears. Each line endpoint becomes a gear; neighbouring gears mesh on the line.
- **Gear Train panel** (click a gear to open it):
  - Module and pressure angle.
  - Teeth per gear, or a ratio per mesh (sets the next gear's teeth).
  - Backlash and tip/root clearance for 3D printing.
  - Bore diameter, and a turn for the first gear.
  - "Set the line lengths to the mesh distance" adds driving dimensions so the gears mesh exactly; each mesh shows ✔ or how far off it is.
- **Meshing:** teeth are turned automatically so neighbouring gears mesh.
- **Extruding:** extrude the sketch; each gear becomes its own body.
